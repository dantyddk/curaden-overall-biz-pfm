Metric definitions & calculation logic

Definitions below reflect the formulas used in this dashboard. All monetary values are shown in EUR.

ORDER SCOPE & COMPARISON

Values are aggregated from Raw by market, shop type, date and hour. Comparison periods use the same hour cutoff. Purchasing user sums column S (Distinct PU); a customer can be counted in more than one hourly row.

GMV_INCTAX

Merchandise value including tax and shipping.

Σ(Line-item quantity × Line-item price) + Shipping
GMV_EXCTAX

GMV after removing tax; sourced from Raw GMV_excTax.

GMV_incTax − Taxes
NET_INCOME_INCTAX

Customer payment remaining after payment-gateway cost.

Actual Customer Paid − Paygate fee
ORDERS & PURCHASING USERS

Orders and purchasing users are summed from hourly Raw rows.

Orders = Σ Orders · PU = Σ Distinct PU (column S)
AVERAGE ORDER VALUE

Average GMV generated by each order.

AOV = Σ GMV_incTax ÷ Σ Orders
% PROMO BURN

Discount cost relative to GMV excluding tax.

Σ Discount Amount ÷ Σ GMV_excTax
PROMO ORDER SHARE

Share of promotional orders. Available when Raw includes Promo Orders.

Σ Promo Orders ÷ Σ Orders
% TAX / GMV INCL. TAX

Tax as a share of GMV including tax.

Σ Tax ÷ Σ GMV_incTax
% PAYGATE FEE

Payment-gateway cost as a share of value after discount.

Σ Paygate fee ÷ Σ(GMV_incTax − Discount)
ITEM PER ORDER

Average quantity purchased per order.

Σ Quantity ÷ Σ Orders
% DISCOUNT / GMV

Discount amount as a share of GMV including tax.

Σ Discount Amount ÷ Σ GMV_incTax
